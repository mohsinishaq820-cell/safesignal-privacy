About SafeSignal

Most online safety advice is written for people who already work in tech. It's full of jargon, buried in fear-mongering, or so generic it doesn't tell you what to actually do. SafeSignal exists to fix that.

What we do

We publish plain-English guides on the everyday privacy and security decisions people actually face — spotting phishing emails, choosing passwords, locking down social media, and knowing what to do after a data breach. No scare tactics, no unnecessary technical detail, just what works and why.

Who it's for

Anyone who uses the internet and wants to feel a little more in control of their own data — whether that's a first pass at securing your accounts, or a specific problem you're trying to solve right now.

Our approach

Every guide is written to be actionable in minutes, not hours. We link to primary sources and current data where it's relevant, and we update guides as threats and platforms change.
